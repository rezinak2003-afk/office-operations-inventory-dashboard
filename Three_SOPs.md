# Three Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

These are practice SOPs and should be aligned to the actual organisation's approval, safety and documentation policies before real use.

## SOP 01 — Receiving Goods
**Purpose:** Confirm delivered items match an approved purchase order and update records accurately.

1. Retrieve the approved purchase order and expected delivery details.
2. Match the supplier, SKU, unit and ordered quantity to the delivery note.
3. Count the physical items; note shortages, damage or substitutions before acceptance.
4. Record a receipt movement as a positive quantity in the Stock Ledger, including source-document reference.
5. Update the received quantity on the PO tracker.
6. Report any variance to the authorised approver and retain the supporting documents.

**Control:** Never mark a PO fully received until the physical count and paperwork agree.

## SOP 02 — Stock Issue / Dispatch
**Purpose:** Keep a traceable record of items issued from inventory.

1. Confirm a valid issue request or dispatch authorisation.
2. Pick the requested SKU and verify item, unit, quantity and recipient/order reference.
3. Confirm sufficient available stock. If the balance would become negative, pause and reconcile first.
4. Record the issue as a negative quantity in the Stock Ledger, with date and reference.
5. Obtain the required acknowledgement and file the dispatch note.
6. Flag items that fall to or below the reorder point.

**Control:** Do not delete a previously recorded movement to conceal an error; document and approve a correction.

## SOP 03 — Daily Records Verification
**Purpose:** Detect common data-quality errors before the records are used for decisions.

1. Check for blank SKUs, duplicate IDs, missing source references and unusual quantities.
2. Review movements with a negative stock balance or unexpected adjustment.
3. Compare physical counts against the workbook for selected high-risk items.
4. Record each variance, investigate the source, and document an approved correction.
5. Review reorder alerts and open purchase orders.
6. Prepare the weekly MIS, add a week-ending date and distribute only to authorised recipients.

**Control:** Preserve an audit trail and store records in the approved location.
