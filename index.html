Business problem and approach

A practical, interview-discussable workflow rather than a decorative dashboard.

The problem

What this addresses

Operations teams need accurate stock balances, traceable movements, purchase-order visibility and a compact weekly report to spot risks early.

The build

What is included

The workbook calculates current stock from signed ledger movements, values inventory, flags reorder points, tracks PO quantities and summarises category-level stock health.

Data integrity

How to interpret it

Sample records are synthetic. Formulas support the workflow, and the workbook includes a usage guide and documented limitations.
